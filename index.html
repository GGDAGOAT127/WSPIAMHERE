OUR MISSION

Stray dogs are commonly caused by abandonment, uncontrolled breeding, and being born on the streets. They often suffer from rabies, infections, skin diseases, and a lack of food, water, and medical care. India recorded around 15.3 million stray dogs in 2019. Cities estimate their populations through surveys and repeated counts. Dogs cause around 99% of human rabies cases globally, making dog bites a serious concern. People can help by adopting dogs, providing food and water, and supporting vaccinations and sterilisation. Our aim is to help in doign so, and joining us is the first step in helping to create a better life for stray dogs
