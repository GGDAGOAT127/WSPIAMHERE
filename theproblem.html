PROBLEM STATEMENT

Stray dogs in India pose a serious challenge to public safety and animal welfare due to overpopulation, lack of food and shelter, inadequate vaccination, and irresponsible pet abandonment, creating an urgent need for humane and effective solutions.

SOME FACTORS

1. Abandonment

Some pet dogs are abandoned by their owners and left to survive on the streets.

2. Lack of Food

Many stray dogs struggle to find enough food and clean drinking water.

3. Lack of Shelter

Dogs are exposed to extreme weather, traffic and other dangers.

4. Diseases and Injuries

Without proper veterinary care, injuries and illnesses can become life-threatening.

5. Overpopulation

Uncontrolled breeding increases the number of dogs living on the streets.
